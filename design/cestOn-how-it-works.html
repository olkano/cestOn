How your folders appear

Codex and Claude keep saved conversation records on the PC. cestOn reads their folder information and combines the results into your recent-work list. It does not move the folders or merge the conversations.

This discovery method was checked against your actual local records. Automatic discovery is not yet part of the installed app.

What stays running

Once the PC has accepted work, closing the phone should not stop it. The PC and the required services need to remain running and connected.

Self-hosted means cestOn runs on your PC. It does not mean the AI model is offline: the configured provider may receive the prompt and relevant context.

A little deeper: the three parts inside cestOn

Coordinator: serves the screen, finds the intended conversation and records commands and progress. Its local database keeps delivery records so reconnecting does not require blindly sending a command again.

Execution broker: talks to the provider through its adapter, checks which session it can control and applies the allowed scope. Provider credentials stay on the host, outside the browser.

Update supervisor: installs verified cestOn updates and handles rollback. It is separate from ordinary conversation work.

These components already exist for managed tasks. Connecting them to your existing live IDE conversations is the unresolved part of the revised workflow.

How does the phone reach the PC?

Through a configured private, authenticated connection, such as a VPN or protected HTTPS endpoint. Pairing the phone identifies it as an allowed device. That setup is separate from choosing a conversation.

A remote-access app for Claude is not meant to be a daily prerequisite. cestOn must establish its own supported provider connection. Access to history alone does not establish that connection.

You should be able to start with a request.

A folder is useful context, not something you should have to remember before asking. Add a permanent New conversation action and a search across all conversations.

“General” is a place in the app

A new conversation can begin under General, with your preferred provider shown before sending. You can ask questions or identify the right project there.

Internally the agent still needs a working directory. cestOn would give it a dedicated app-owned folder. This would not grant access to all your repositories or relocate your files.

Find first, then act

Search all indexed titles and folder names without choosing a project. Searching message content would need an explicit, bounded scope; the existing discovery snapshot contains no messages.

If several results match, cestOn should show the choices and their evidence. If changing the working folder requires a new provider session, it should explain that before starting one.

These are proposed interactions. No Home Assistant integration, cross-conversation content search or General conversation flow has been verified in cestOn.

Would the response stream?

That is the proposed experience where the provider supplies partial text: the answer grows in this panel. If it supplies a whole message at once, that message appears when it arrives.

The current code receives Codex text fragments and Claude assistant messages, and has a live event channel. The current screen does not yet assemble them into a verified chat stream for existing sessions.

What about “thoughts”?

Show useful progress, such as “Looking for matching conversations,” “Reading a file,” or “Waiting for your approval,” when supported by actual events.

Provider-supplied explanations or reasoning summaries could be optional details. A full private thought process is not something cestOn can promise to display. No extra “thinking” stream is required by this proposal.

What if the connection drops just after Send?

If the PC already received the command, work may continue while the phone is disconnected. On reconnect, cestOn should look up that same delivery record and recover progress. It must not automatically send a second copy just because the acknowledgement was lost.

If the command was never received, it remains unsent. Show “Checking delivery” while the outcome is uncertain, and preserve the draft. Saving a draft is not the same as putting a command into an automatic queue.

What if the agent is already working?

Show whether the new message will wait for the next turn or can be delivered immediately. The choice depends on the provider connection. A message marked “Queued for next turn” has not interrupted the current work.

Do not imply that all providers support interruption or mid-response instructions in the same way.

What if the agent needs me, or the action fails?

The conversation should show the question or required approval, with the affected target and action. A failure should appear there too, with a clear explanation. The full same-conversation interaction remains to be tested.

“Received” means the PC has the command. “Agent accepted” means the provider has it. Neither means the requested action succeeded. For something like a restart, a final result needs a follow-up check of the target.

Two different connections can fail.

The old “PC offline” wording was too definite. A phone often knows only that it cannot reach cestOn, not whether the computer is switched off.

Conversation-specific problem

Your PC is reachable. cestOn can read the saved history, but has no supported connection to send into that particular conversation. Other conversations may still work.

Waking the PC will not resolve an unsupported session connection. cestOn should show the exact limitation and any supported next step.

Phone-to-PC problem

The phone cannot reach cestOn. The PC may be asleep, cestOn may have stopped, or the phone/network/private connection may be unavailable.

Fresh updates and sending are unavailable for all conversations on that PC. Only content already cached on the phone could remain readable. This cache is still an acceptance requirement.

There can also be a provider outage or login problem while the PC is reachable. That should say “Codex unavailable” or “Claude unavailable,” with the known reason. None of these states means the conversation was deleted.

What is proven today? Local discovery and the illustrative navigation have been checked. The managed-task architecture exists. Same-session phone replies, General conversations, cross-project search and a polished streaming conversation view still need implementation and real-provider/device acceptance.
